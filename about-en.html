About the Artist

Ink, Virtue
& Stillness

Rooted in the classical tradition of Chinese ink painting and calligraphy, this artist's practice begins where landscape, history, and emotion converge — where brushwork becomes a language of inner tranquility and strength.

Deeply influenced from an early age by the mountains-and-water paintings of the literati tradition, the artist has spent decades exploring what becomes possible when ancient forms meet contemporary consciousness.

Each work carries a dual nature: the measured breath of classical aesthetics, and the living pulse of personal expression. The invitation is to feel, not merely to see.
